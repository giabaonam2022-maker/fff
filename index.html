Happy Vietnamese Teachers’ Day

20 November

Dear Ms. Hạnh, this little card was made with a grateful heart. Please sit back, let the music play, and read the words I have been saving for you, my English teacher, on this special day.

Dear Ms. Hạnh,

Today is the 20th of November, the day when all of Vietnam says thank you to its teachers. I would like to say it to you, in the very language you taught me, because you deserve every word of it.

Thank you for the words

When I first walked into your class, English felt like a locked door. You did not simply hand me the key; you showed me how to turn it. Little by little, you gave me the words, and then the courage to use them.

Thank you for your patience

I made many mistakes in front of you, and you never made me feel small. You smiled, you corrected me gently, and you said, “Try again.” Those two words have taught me more than any grammar book ever could.

More than English

From you, I learned to listen before I speak, to stay kind even when I disagree, and to remain curious about the world. These lessons are not on any exam, but I use them every single day of my life.

Your little garden

Your classroom was never just four walls and a whiteboard. It was a small garden where every student could grow at their own pace, and you were the gardener who watered us with patience, laughter, and endless belief.

My promise to you

I promise to keep learning, to keep reading, and to keep speaking English with confidence, even when it feels difficult. Whenever I succeed, a part of that success will always belong to you, because you were one of the first people to believe in me.

My wishes for you

I wish you good health, a peaceful heart, and a life full of laughter. May every class you teach be bright, may every student you meet remember you as warmly as I do, and may every dream of yours bloom like a flower in spring.

Happy Teachers’ Day, Ms. Hạnh!

Thank you for every lesson, every smile, and every moment you gave us. Wherever life takes me, I will always remember you with love and respect, and I will always be proud to say that I was your student.

With all my love and gratitude, your student
